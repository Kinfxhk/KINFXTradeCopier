# Known issues and evidence limits

This source alpha is open for collaboration **with trading disabled**. These issues block later simulator/provider acceptance, not publication of disarmed source and synthetic tests. No live readiness, automatic protection recovery or merged-candidate acceptance is claimed.

Historical source/candidate hashes and scoped counts are in [EVIDENCE.json](EVIDENCE.json); the missing-test plan is [TEST-MATRIX.md](TEST-MATRIX.md). Findings below attach to their recorded implementations. The public baseline-derived snapshot has publication-hold edits, not the repair clones; this audit did not execute its engine. Reproduce against the exact current source before closing an issue.

## P0 safety categories

### F01 — protective resizing can issue an unsafe intermediate request

**Known baseline product defect; later scratch frontier controls pass, not integrated.** [E02, E03]

Synthetic reproduction:
1. Maintain acknowledged protective total4 and captured positive fill2 while the mutable wrapper is already Filled4.
2. Report Position1 while owned execution evidence is2; the baseline requests total3 instead of preserving/quarantining the total4 protection.
3. Separately queue older Working0 before PartFilled2. Baseline requests total2 and later total4. The final correction cannot undo the earlier external request.

`FreshPositionAheadDoesNotUndersize` and `FreshLateWorkingBeforePartialCannotUndersize` both failed, each repeated five times on two copies with the same baseline hash. A later clone's core16 passes those controls; that does not certify existing broker coverage, final public bytes or all ACK interleavings.

**Public repo status (source `b13bda04…`):** a narrow **quantity freeze**, not the frontier design. While Position reads lower than captured owned fills, the engine will not shrink any acknowledged exit quantity; price moves still go through; it does **not** latch, lock or cancel anything. A real leader reduction is applied once Position agrees with the owned ledger again.
**Property sweep added (offline, synthetic; no source change):** `FrontierPropertySweep` enumerates 150 combinations of captured fill (0 or 2), a provider fill that is applied or still queued (0, 1 or 2 lots), Position reading (0 to 4) and a leader amend to 1, 2 or 4, and `TwoExitsAmendedUpNeverOverCover` amends two independent 2+2 exits upward on 4 held lots under four Position readings. They check on every outbound Change and on the final state: no Change below the acknowledged quantity while Position lags, non-terminal exit quantity never above the true filled lots, and convergence to min(leader, true filled) once evidence catches up. **All pass on the current engine, so no new defect was found.** That does not mean Issue #2 is solved: the engine still has only the quantity freeze, not the captured/applied frontier, one-unresolved-command rule or ACK interleaving, and the sweep drives a fake whose Position can only lag, never lead. The sweep has teeth: removing the freeze turns it red (21 violating combinations), as do the five existing F01 cases; removing the other-exit reservation turns `TwoExitsAmendedUpNeverOverCover` red (working 8 on 4 lots).
- Before: with a stop acknowledged at 4, owned fills 4 and Position reading 1, the engine sent `Change(total 1)` (RED, `F01PositionBehindOwnedNeverShrinksProtection`).
- An independent reviewer found my first version (lock + no change) was wrong in three ways, all reproduced here and now covered by tests: two independent stops 2+2 with Position 3 still shrank one stop to 1 (`F01TwoIndependentExitsNeverShrinkOnPositionLag`, base and first version both failed); a price-only leader move was dropped (`F01PriceMoveStillForwardedDuringPositionLag`); a transient lag permanently latched the account (`F01PositionLagDoesNotLockOrCancel`). `F01ReductionAppliedAfterPositionCatchesUp` checks the freeze is not permanent.
- **Accepted trade-off, not solved:** during a lag the exit can be larger than the real position. If the cause is a genuine external reduction, a triggered exit can reverse the account. Quantity freeze favours "never under-protect" over "never over-protect". See `docs/DO-NOT-DO.md`.
- **Not a policy the reviewer settled:** with no lag at all, the follower mirrors a leader reduction from 4 to 1 (reviewer R10). That is existing design, not covered by this guard; whether it should be limited to owned exposure is an open decision.
- Scenario 2 (older Working0 queued before PartFilled2) already passed on this baseline in the public fake (`F01QueuedStaleWorkingNeverUndersizes`); this does not prove it either way. No captured/applied frontier, no one-unresolved-command rule, ACK interleavings untested.

**Task:** preserve captured/applied account frontiers, requested/acknowledged/reserved totals and one unresolved command; assert every request under delayed/no ACK and accepted-then-throw. [Issue #2](https://github.com/Kinfxhk/KINFXTradeCopier/issues/2)

### OCO — completed group versus account-wide remaining exposure

**Known residual product defect on tested repair clones; no group-completion implementation acceptance.** [E03, E10]

Synthetic reproduction: allocate entry exposure4 to groups A2 and B2. Fill A's target2 completely while B still owns2. A's stop should be retired only with group-scoped completion proof; B's protection must remain. `ReviewOcoCompletedSliceCancelOnlyItsGroup` remains FAIL on frozen/cancellation candidates (OCO10 9 PASS / 1 FAIL each). Account-wide owned-zero cannot distinguish this case; guessed group allocation can cancel the sole valid stop.

Also retain pending-amendment completion, partial terminal target, unequal sole-stop, late entry and OCO double-fill cases. Largest-member OCO budgeting is not a simultaneous-fill bound or broker reduce-only guarantee. Pure certificate/allocation algebra is research, not a production repair or native ATM implementation.

**Task:** define stable slice allocation and immutable group/session/frontier certificates, then test all emitted cancellations and both-side double fills without dropping positive evidence. [Issue #3](https://github.com/Kinfxhk/KINFXTradeCopier/issues/3)

### Mailbox, public Dispose and head-of-line blocking

**Partly addressed in the public repo (source `db55c8ca…`):** `SerialWorker.Post` now returns whether the work was accepted. Four call sites raised state before Post and relied on the posted action to clear it, so a stopped mailbox left them stuck: the pending follower-callback counter (`CanClose` could never succeed), the poll-pending flag (every later Poll skipped), the follow request generation (UI row pending forever) and `suppressNew` in the fake-only Flatten path. Each was reproduced RED in `EngineSafetyTests` and is now GREEN: `RefusedFollowerCallbackDoesNotStrandPendingCounter`, `RefusedPollDoesNotStickPollPending`, `RefusedSetFollowingDoesNotStrandProcessedGeneration`, `RefusedFlattenDoesNotStickSuppressNew`; `RiskTests.WorkerRetention` covers accepted-work retention and refusal reporting. An independent reviewer reproduced RED on the base and GREEN on the new source, and found the last two call sites, which are now fixed. **Not addressed:** one stalled follower blocking healthy followers (head-of-line), Dispose across multiple drain turns, a `thread.Join(3000)` timeout leaving accepted tasks unrun (static reading only), and real NinjaTrader callback threading. A fill callback refused by a stopped mailbox is not applied to the ledger by design: `ledgerUnresolved` stays raised, which is fail-closed; the reviewer found it does not deadlock a detached session, but captured fill evidence for that one callback is lost once the session is gone.



**Known fake-engine failures; real multi-account latency remains untested.** [E10, E13]

Synthetic reproduction:
- Admit callbacks during a blocked cleanliness read in public Dispose; span multiple bounded drain turns with 130 positive envelopes. Owner evidence retained64 and stranded66. A pre-read captured==applied check does not certify post-read cleanliness.
- Stop the worker and Post again: rejection must be observable rather than silently accepted/discarded.
- Block one follower's Submit or Change while 10/20/30 followers share the worker. Measure healthy-follower progress and OFF/cancellation delay; a drain batch limit is not mailbox capacity or worker isolation.

`RequestMailboxHardBound`, `RequestRejectedPostMustBeObservable` and `RequestPublicDisposeCannotLoseAcceptedCallbacks` remain FAIL. Historical scale130 is 63 PASS / 67 FAIL, including blocked-call/HOL scenarios; those are overlapping invocations, not 67 independent defect categories. Later ON/cancellation frontier repairs explicitly did not fix mailbox/Dispose/HOL.

**Task:** lossless bounded admission, explicit refusal/continuation semantics, no false clean detach, and isolated slow-account execution with deterministic barriers. Do not fix overload by dropping fill payloads. [Issue #4](https://github.com/Kinfxhk/KINFXTradeCopier/issues/4)

### Settings drafts, identity membership and publication contracts

**Partly addressed in the public repo (source `e39b39e9…`):** the two editable amount columns, Target and DLL, now keep a row-owned text draft. A malformed hand-typed value (`12x5`, `5oo`, `1,000`) stays on screen after a broad refresh, still blocks commit/persist after the row was scrolled out of view, and never overwrites the last valid number; the model also rejects negative, NaN and Infinity. Covered by `BadTargetDraftSurvivesRefreshAndBlocksCommit`, `BadLossDraftSurvivesRefreshAndBlocksCommit`, `BadTargetDraftSurvivesRecyclingAndStillBlocksCommit`, `TargetAndLossSetterRejectInvalidNumbers`. **Still open:** the other numeric fields (Floor, Baseline, Trail, Max, ...) in the Risk dialog have no row-owned drafts; hidden accepted-state mutation across ALL rows, XML precision of Target/DLL, `-0` and BaselineSet, and the settings publication contract are unchanged. Synthetic setters and a scripted grid edit are not proof of what a user's real keyboard path does in a live NinjaTrader window.



**Scoped membership repair passes; not integrated. Publication failures need policy adjudication.** [E03, E05]

Synthetic reproduction: add the same AccountRow reference twice, remove one occurrence, then rename/export the remaining visible row. A unique-identity HashSet can lose ownership while an occurrence remains. Candidate alias4 and collection22 pass, but this does not accept all native draft/token paths.

For nested Export/Persist/Save, an observer can finish a valid inner publication before invalidating the outer token. Six broad unchanged-everything failures include that already-completed inner operation. They cannot automatically be called outer corruption. Fresh run2 has 41 PASS / 14 FAIL: six broad oracles, six proposed whole-root policy controls, one strict-zero-transient visibility control and one representation-only XML-ordering failure. The latter retains its runtime FAIL despite equal field maps and unchanged disk/index bytes. No whole-root policy was adopted.

**Task:** agree operation-local refusal versus whole-resource isolation; preserve valid completed inner work unless the contract forbids it. Validate all row-owned numeric drafts, actual recycling, selected identity revisions, modal tokens and exact persistence methods. [Issue #5](https://github.com/Kinfxhk/KINFXTradeCopier/issues/5)

### Journal admission, callback reentry and recovery truth

**Standalone fixes pass their scope; not integrated. Nested Dispose policy FAIL and provider closure unknown remain.** [E04, E06, E07]

Synthetic reproductions:
- An existing-latch or committed-proposal idempotent shortcut can acknowledge malformed UTF-16 without codec admission.
- Configured process-local SHA callbacks can reenter before frame admission, producing acknowledged duplicate sequence/predecessor frames which do not all replay.
- An internal synthetic verifier mutates revision during Prepare/Commit; a stale proof must be refused before proposal/frame publication, not after writing an invalid tail.

Early-admission fixes alone leave three minimal reentry failures. The three-fix candidate has 908 PASS / 3 FAIL among 911 final-candidate invocations; the encompassing 25-run/948 ledger also includes RED/intermediate/base runs. Independent fresh23 is 23 PASS versus original 5 PASS / 18 FAIL; retained20 remains 17 PASS / 3 nested Dispose release-expectation FAIL. Those failures concern the documented policy requiring a later explicit Dispose rather than immediate release from a nested fault callback; do not conflate them with engine Dispose callback loss.

Custom-crypto/internal-verifier probes do not prove default production reachability. A synthetic committed proposal, hash chain, Position0 or all visible orders terminal does not authenticate closure of a hidden accepted Flatten request. The baseline XML risk-latch save tests are not a durable execution-recovery journal.

**Task:** define nested-disposal contract, authenticated scoped provider/operator evidence and every ambiguous-request resolution; integrate versioned recovery, restart, rotation and power-loss tests while preserving authorization latches and original fills. [Issue #6](https://github.com/Kinfxhk/KINFXTradeCopier/issues/6)

### Per-account TP / SL after hand-tuning (not implemented)
Follower TP / SL are mirrors of the leader's non-market exits. With ATM off a follower has no TP / SL of its own yet still copies a leader **market** exit, so a hand-tuned account is dragged out when the leader flattens. There is no self-managed exit mode or per-account TP / SL distance. Depends on #2, #3 and #9; do not add follower-owned protective orders first. [Issue #10](https://github.com/Kinfxhk/KINFXTradeCopier/issues/10)

### Numeric drafts (Issue #5): grid cells now keep typed text; the rest is still open
**Fixed (source `3e87924a…`):** Target and DLL already kept a typed draft. **Ratio** and **Max** did not: a mistyped Ratio such as `1.5x` was replaced by the old value on the next refresh, and Max accepted `0` or a negative number, which made the row's settings invalid. Both now follow the Target/DLL rules: the typed text stays visible through refresh and row recycling, shows a validation error, blocks commit and persist, and the accepted value stays the last valid one. Max must be a whole number of at least 1; Ratio a plain positive number (no commas, signs or text). Tests: `BadRatioDraftSurvivesRefreshAndBlocksCommit`, `MaxContractsRejectsInvalidAndKeepsDraft`; removing any of the four pieces (Ratio bound to the number, no draft flag, Max accepting zero, Max bound to the number) turns one of them red. The Risk dialog validates all its fields together and refuses the whole form on a bad value, so it has no half-committed state.
**Not addressed:** (1) the Risk dialog's other fields (Floor, Baseline, Trail, High water, Best day, Total profit, Consistency) are checked only when you press Apply, not as you type, and are not kept as drafts across a refresh; (2) bulk "From Leader" and bulk Tgt/DLL validate before they change any row, but a partial in-memory commit failure across many rows has no test beyond that; (3) Target high-precision XML round-trip, `0,125` read as `125`, and signed-zero Baseline: still open from the earlier review; (4) real native window focus, IME input and clipboard paste are not exercised.

### Head-of-line blocking (Issue #4): per-account provider dispatch merged, with limits
**Fixed (source `a3196c7e…`):** every follower provider call (Submit, Change, Cancel, Flatten) used to run inline on the single engine thread, so ONE follower stalled inside a provider call froze all others. Each follower now has its own serial dispatch thread; engine state is still changed only on the engine thread, and a call's result is posted back to it. Per account the order is preserved (one thread per account), `CanClose` and `Dispose` refuse while any provider call is unresolved, and a Flatten queues behind any Submit/Change/Cancel already queued for the same account. Tests: `StalledFollowerDoesNotBlockHealthyOnes` (10, 20 and 30 followers, one blocked in Submit), `StalledFollowerDoesNotBlockCancelOfOthers`, `StalledProviderCallBlocksCloseUntilResolved`, `StalledCancelBlocksCloseUntilResolved`, `ThrowingFollowerLocksOnlyItself`, `PerAccountCommandOrderIsPreserved`, `PerAccountOrderUnderBurst`.
**Three defects an independent reviewer found in my first version, all fixed before publication, each with a test that fails without the fix:** (1) a Flatten overtook a Submit already queued for the same account and left a working Sell on a flat account (`FlattenDoesNotOvertakeQueuedSubmit`); (2) after one provider call for an account threw, an entry decided before the failure reached the engine thread was still sent (`SubmitThrowStillSendsNoSecondEntry`, now an `account.ProviderFaulted` gate); (3) a leader revert to the previous price was dropped as "no change" while an earlier Change was still queued (`RevertAfterQueuedChangeIsStillSent`). **A fourth I introduced while fixing them and found myself:** comparing against the in-flight request made the exit quantity oscillate between two values on every poll (600 Change calls, test timeout); fixed by freezing against the in-flight quantity, `AmendedExitDoesNotOscillateOnPoll`.
**Not addressed:** (1) the design is verified only against a fake that lets a call block; real NinjaTrader `Account.Submit/Change/Cancel` thread-affinity and re-entrancy are unverified, and it is possible that NinjaTrader requires these calls on a specific thread; (2) `Dispose` still waits at most 3 s per dispatcher, and a call that never returns is abandoned; (3) a stale completion that arrives after the session ended is dropped by the session check but has not been attacked by a dedicated test; (4) a Change whose acceptance is not yet confirmed is still skipped (older behaviour); (5) the per-account order of DIFFERENT commands is preserved, but ordering across accounts for one leader action is not defined and was not tested beyond OFF/cancel; (6) hold: every real Account is still blocked, so none of this ran against a provider.

### Group OCO completion (Issue #3): narrow fix merged, allocation still open
**Fixed (source `f5ff5aca…`):** when one OCO group completed, the engine cancelled the sole protection of another group that was still open. Cause (found with a probe): the completed group's stop stays "Working" in captured evidence until its cancel is acknowledged, so it still counted as reserving the exit quantity and the other group looked like it had nothing to close. The engine now marks a sibling it retired because its OCO group completed (`CopyLink.OcoRetired`) and such a sibling stops reserving capacity for a different group. A leader-cancelled exit is **not** marked, because its provider cancel may still be unacknowledged and it must keep reserving, or two working exits could cover the same lots.
A first version of this fix filtered on the general `Cancelled` flag. An independent review showed that was a regression (working exit quantity above the lots held when a leader-cancelled exit was unacknowledged, never acknowledged, or partly filled). It was replaced before anything was published; the three tests that catch it are `UnackedCancelledExitDifferentOcoStillReserves`, `UnackedCancelledExitNeverAckedStillReserves` and `UnackedCancelledPartiallyFilledExitStillReserves`.
**Policy decided by the maintainer (conservative):** keep locking the account and **never** create orders automatically; instead say so plainly. When an exit-completion lock fires, the reason now ends with "N lots UNPROTECTED (no protective exit working) — check NinjaTrader now and place or flatten by hand; this copier will not create it". N is the owned position minus the remaining quantity of this copier's own working, non-cancelled exits, so a cancelled exit counts as covering nothing and a fully covered account is never told it is unprotected. Tests: `UnprotectedLotsFigureIsExact`, `UnprotectedLotsTextIsExactAndNeverOptimistic`, `UnprotectedReasonReachesRowAndDetail`; forcing the figure to always report all lots, never report any, or count a cancelled exit as cover each turns a test red.
**The gap itself is not closed:** those lots stay without a protective exit until a human acts. This only makes the warning exact and impossible to misread.
**Not addressed:** (1) if an OCO group completes while the entry still has unfilled lots, a later fill cannot be assigned to a group safely, so the engine locks the account for manual recovery, **and the waiting exit of the other group is never sent: those lots are unprotected until you act** (`OcoCompletionWithUnfilledEntryFailsClosed` documents this limitation, it is not a safe outcome); (2) fractional allocation of exit quantity across groups, which needs an entry-lot to group allocation certificate; (3) a same-OCO-id leader-cancelled exit that is unacknowledged is still not covered by a test (older behaviour, not changed); (4) real NinjaTrader cancel-acknowledgement timing.

### Divergence reaction (Issue #12): setting stored, behaviour not built
Each account has an `OnDivergence` choice, `Notify` (default) or `FlattenOnce`, saved per account and carried by Bulk and From Leader. It is **stored only**: the engine never flattens by itself, Flatten is disabled for every provider, and a row set to `FlattenOnce` shows a note saying so. What actually happens on divergence is still: lock, red state, red footer count, log line. An automatic single flatten needs the uncertain-Flatten handling above and real provider evidence first.

## Keep non-product failures visible

- **Pure SPEC:** final validator388 and contract191 PASS do not exercise the C# engine. semantic91 still has 89 PASS / 2 schema FAIL (`register_schema_session`, `register_schema_generation`). Registration-price hints, Cancel capacity and ACKs below positive fills have scoped validator corrections, not integrated dispatch acceptance. The preserved initial harness failures and corrected legal-prefix fixtures must remain separately identified. [E08, E09]
- **Harness:** nonexistent/misordered reflection method invocation, missing fixture-relative seed files, output file locks and compiler-host crashes can fail before the intended assertion. Preserve these attempts; corrected invocation is not a product RED→GREEN. One secondary fake callback legitimately advanced the exit ledger in an unisolated probe; isolated and unisolated results are both retained. [E01, E10]
- **Fixture callback gap (fixed in tests, not product):** `AtmMode`, `Lifecycle` and `SubsetFlatten` marked the fake follower entry Filled/Position but never emitted that fill as an `OrderUpdate`. The engine correctly refuses to copy exits across an unobserved fill, so they timed out. Each now emits the fill callback (3 added `Emit` calls, no assertion changed).
- **Policy decided, Issue #9:** the two remaining engine failures were old expectations that conflicted with the safety policy. The maintainer decided: (1) a flat Position reading while the engine still owns exposure is ambiguous telemetry and must **not** cancel the only protective exit (lock, keep the exit); (2) after a **thrown Flatten** the engine must **not** copy a new leader exit onto that account and must **not** retry the Flatten (it may already have been accepted); lock and notify. `FlatWorkingExits` and `FailedFlattenKeepsProtection` in `EngineTests.cs` now assert exactly that, with the superseded assertions kept as comments. This changed the oracle, not the engine: the engine already behaved this way. **Cost, stated plainly:** after a thrown Flatten the account can hold a new position with no copied exit until you reconcile by hand. Decided trade-off, not a solved problem.
- **Pure performance policy:** G01 index review retains 333 PASS / 35 FAIL; equivalent snapshots and deterministic node/scan budgets are not provider latency acceptance. A prior scope incident remains separate from technical findings; a clean parent replay does not erase it. [E11]
- **Build tooling:** a scratch standalone driver passes 18 boundary probes and one NT compile-only. It does not fix PowerShell Roslyn StackOverflow root cause, long-path limitations or bypass through direct editor/compiler builds. Source and reference warnings have different origins; warning counts are not defect counts. [E12; issue #1](https://github.com/Kinfxhk/KINFXTradeCopier/issues/1)

## Unknown acceptance, not a solved defect

No controlled acceptance with **two or more actual NT demo accounts** is established. Real provider identity replacement, ordering/reconnect/late fills, actual execution slippage, unknown Submit/Change/Cancel/Flatten acceptance and OCO double fills remain unknown. NT shutdown/workspace detach, thirty-account real latency/HOL, integrated authenticated journal restart/rotation/power loss, true per-monitor DPI and native modal draft/token lifetime remain untested. [Future simulator design #7](https://github.com/Kinfxhk/KINFXTradeCopier/issues/7), [native UI/DPI #8](https://github.com/Kinfxhk/KINFXTradeCopier/issues/8)

Do not unlock trading by editing a define or removing a hold. A later simulator design needs explicit authorization, reviewed exact source and measurable abort/reconciliation criteria. Live operation is outside this alpha's acceptance.
